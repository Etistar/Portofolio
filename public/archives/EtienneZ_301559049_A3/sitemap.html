Site Map

  • Home Page (home.html) - Main page displaying the restaurant's picture, mission statement, and customer feedback.
  • About Page (about.html) - Additional history and details about the restaurant.
  • Menu (Dropdown Navigation):
    • Calendar Page (calendar.html) - Restaurant events and schedule details.
    • Gallery Page (gallery.html) - Photos of dishes and restaurant atmosphere.
    • Menu List Page (menulist.html) - Display of names, images, and prices for Dine-In or Take-Away items.
  • Order Page (order.html) - Customer ordering page showing chosen items and total cost with 13% tax.
  • Location Page (location.html) - Address, map, and restaurant accessibility information.
  • Contact Page (contact.html) - Contact form alongside phone, email, Facebook, and Twitter handles.
  • Site Map and Credits (sitemap.html) - This current page.
