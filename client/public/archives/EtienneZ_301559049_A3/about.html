Our Story

Established in 2021, The Progress Restaurant began as a small family-owned kitchen dedicated to serving communities in the Ottawa-Gatineau region [2]. Over the years, we have grown into a culinary destination celebrated for blending traditional cooking styles with modern culinary innovation, delivering authentic experiences straight to your table.

Our Vision & Core Values

Culinary Integrity: We ensure that every plate contains only high-quality, fresh ingredients sourced directly from dependable local producers and farmers.

Community Warmth: Creating an open environment where friends and families feel right at home with attentive, cheerful hospitality.

Continuous Innovation: Blending cultural flavors from Italian and Indian traditions to continuously surprise and delight your palate.
